§01

Overview

Triage, not accuracy maximization

Crash severity models rarely control what data was available at decision time, rarely report how badly they under-triage severe cases, and rarely say when they should defer to a person instead of guessing.

This study reframes dementia-related crash severity modeling as a decision-aware triage problem: classify each crash into no-injury/property-damage-only (O), minor or moderate injury (BC), or fatal/severe injury (KA) — while controlling outcome leakage, reporting severe under-triage, calibrating confidence, and preserving every raw prediction for audit.

RQ1

Do police narratives improve KA / BC / O classification beyond structured variables alone?

RQ2

How much performance survives after removing structured severity leakage and masking outcome-revealing narrative terms?

RQ3

Does calibrated deferral reduce severe under-triage — especially KA crashes classified as BC or O?

Leakage control

22 seed terms + up to 75 corpus-derived phrases masked from narratives before evaluation.

Calibrated uncertainty

Temperature scaling drops expected calibration error from 0.089 to 0.033 without changing labels.

Selective deferral

Withholding low-confidence cases raises retained-case macro-F1 from 0.522 to 0.573 at 70% coverage.

Full audit trail

Every split, prompt, response, probability, and log preserved for traceable, reproducible review.

01 · Data Ingestion — Texas CRIS crash records (2017–2025, ~5M records) are searched with predefined dementia-related terms, then manually reviewed for sufficient narrative evidence. The final analytical dataset retains 4,781 crashes with 25 structured predictors plus narratives.

Cost-sensitive learning

Separates probability estimates from decision rules under unequal error costs (Elkan, 2001) — KA→O errors cost 5×, KA→BC cost 3×.

Selective classification

Allows abstention when confidence is insufficient (Geifman & El-Yaniv, 2017), routing uncertain crashes toward human review.

Public-sector accountability

Deployment introduces accountability obligations (Busuioc, 2021) — model size affects who can run, audit, and update the system.

Data & Experimental Design

4,781 Texas dementia-related crash records

Dementia status is crash-record reported, not a clinically verified diagnosis. A fixed stratified 70/15/15 split is used throughout.

Gemma under-triages 27 of 73 true KA records (8 as O, 19 as BC) while also over-predicting KA for many non-KA cases — a more severe-case-sensitive operating point rather than uniform improvement, better suited to screening than fully automated classification.

Early–late fusion agreement

83.3% label agreement, but mean absolute probability distance of 0.084 — late fusion mainly changes boundary-case uncertainty.

Confidence gap

Correct calibrated-fusion predictions average confidence 0.622, vs. 0.562 for errors — supporting the deferral rule.

Cost-matrix sensitivity

Comparing lower-asymmetry, baseline, and higher-severe-miss cost scenarios changes cost by −11.2% to +9.5% for direct prediction, but the 80%-coverage deferral benefit stays directionally stable (−9.2% to −12.3%) across all three.

§08

Limitations & Future Work

Reading the results with appropriate caution

Dementia status is record-reported, not clinical+
Candidate cases were identified via high-recall term search plus manual contextual review of narratives — this is crash-record-reported information, not a clinically verified diagnosis.
Leakage terms are dataset-specific+
The masking procedure targets identified outcome-revealing terms and corpus-derived phrases, but resulting inputs are "leakage-controlled" rather than provably free of all outcome proxies.
Single split, no repeated-split uncertainty+
Model rankings are based on one fixed stratified 70/15/15 split and are presented without confidence intervals for repeated-split variation — they are conditional on this evaluation split.
Confidence-only deferral rule+
The deferral rule uses calibrated confidence alone. Future work should combine confidence, expected severity cost, class-specific uncertainty, and analyst capacity in deferral policy.
No reviewer-in-the-loop evaluation+
Deferred cases are treated as candidates for human review but are not further evaluated — reviewer accuracy, workload, disagreement, response time, and post-review outcomes are not measured in this study.
Small, specialized, imbalanced dataset+
Transformer baselines used a relatively small imbalanced dataset; local LLM results depend on deterministic prompting and model availability. Generalizability to broader older-driver or medical-condition crash populations may be limited.
